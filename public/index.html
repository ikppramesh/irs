              Why it costs less here

              • Fares shown are the official IRCTC fare (base + reservation + superfast + GST). IRS adds nothing.
              • Third-party apps often add ₹20–₹100+ per ticket as "convenience" or "agent" fees. Booking directly on IRCTC skips that.
              • IRCTC itself charges a small convenience fee at payment (about ₹15 for non-AC, ₹30 for AC, plus GST) and your bank/UPI gateway may add a little. UPI is usually cheapest.
              • Unreserved (general) tickets can be bought on the UTS app with no convenience fee.
